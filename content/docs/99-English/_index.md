### ***My suggestions for you (B1 level + AI topic)***

***Stick with Bob the Canadian for warm-up listening (3–4 times a week)***

His videos build your ear for natural speed and clear pronunciation. Good ones to connect a little to "future/modern life":
"Let's Learn English! Topic: The Future" (if he has one like this, or similar about plans/dreams)
Videos about "technology" or "phones/computers" if you find any short ones.
Keep using subtitles first, then without — this makes your brain stronger for harder topics like AI.

Best channels for AI topics at intermediate level
These have clear English, subtitles, and speak not too fast. They explain new words (like algorithm, machine learning, chatbot, predict, ethical...). Start with 0.75x–0.9x speed if needed.

***BBC Learning English (highly recommended for B1)***

They have short, excellent videos/podcasts about AI.
Examples:
"6 Minute English: Training artificial intelligence" — talks about how smart AI is (like earthworm level? 😄), misunderstandings, and training AI. Very clear, with transcript!
Search their channel for "AI" or "artificial intelligence" — many 6-minute episodes on future of AI, jobs, ethics.
→ Routine: Listen once → read transcript → listen again → note 5–10 new words (e.g., "trial and error", "brain cell", "misunderstanding").

***VOA Learning English (Voice of America)***

Slow, clear American English + news style.
They have videos on AI in daily life, self-driving cars, robots.
Search: "AI" or "artificial intelligence" on their channel. Great for listening to real news but simplified.

***EnglishClass101.com or mmmEnglish***

Sometimes they cover modern topics like "How AI is changing jobs" or "ChatGPT explained".
Look for intermediate playlists.

***TED-Ed or short TED Talks (with subtitles)***

Search "AI for beginners" or "What is artificial intelligence?" — many animated, visual videos (5–10 min) that explain simply.
Good for visual + listening (pictures help understand hard ideas).

***Your weekly practice plan for AI + listening:***
- Day 1–2: Watch 1 Bob the Canadian video (any topic) → build confidence.
- Day 3–4: Watch 1 BBC 6 Minute English about AI → use transcript.
- Day 5: Try a VOA or TED-Ed video on AI → no speed change, subtitles off second time.
- Repeat hard sentences out loud (shadowing).
- New words list: Write AI words like "intelligence", "robot", "predict", "data", "future", "ethical", "replace jobs" — then use them in sentences.

Extra fun tip
After a few weeks, try asking ChatGPT (or me!) simple questions in English like: "Explain AI in simple English for B1 level." Then read/listen to the answer and practice repeating.

AI topics will help your vocabulary grow fast because they're everywhere now (news, work, phones...). In 1–2 months, you'll understand a lot more!

### ***Your Practice Plan & Suggestions:***
- Step 1: Choose the Right Videos
Start with his older, simpler videos. Look for these playlists or keywords:

"Learn English in..." videos (e.g., "Learn English at the Grocery Store").

"English Lessons with..." videos where he teaches in a specific location.

"Slow and Easy English" playlist (if he has one).

Avoid very long (20+ min) or topic-specific videos (like advanced grammar) at first.

- Step 2: Active Listening Strategy (Do NOT just watch passively)
Use the "Three-Phase" method for each video:

First Watch (With Subtitles):

Watch the video with English subtitles (click the [CC] button on YouTube).

Don't stop. Try to understand the main idea. Just listen, read, and enjoy.

Second Watch (Active Focus):

Watch again. Pause often.

Write down 3-5 new words or phrases you heard. Look them up.

Try to repeat sentences after him. This helps your pronunciation and memory.

Ask yourself: "Can I explain what he just said in my own simple words?"

Third Watch (Without Subtitles):

Turn the subtitles OFF.

See how much you can understand just by listening now. You will be surprised!

Focus on the sounds, his intonation (the music of his sentences), and linking words.

- Step 3: After Watching
Use the New Vocabulary: Try to make 2-3 new sentences with the words you wrote down. Say them out loud or write them in a notebook.

***Summarize:*** Tell a friend, a language partner, or even yourself (in the mirror!) what the video was about. Use simple sentences: "Bob walked through a park. He described the trees and the weather. He used words like 'path', 'bench', and 'foliage'."

***Regular Practice:*** Little and often is better than a lot once a week. Try for 15-20 minutes, 4-5 times a week.

- Extra Tips for Success:
Use YouTube Tools: You can slow down the video speed (Settings → Playback Speed → 0.75x) if you need to. But try normal speed first, as Bob already speaks clearly.

Engage with the Community: Read the comments. Often, other learners ask questions or share notes. You can write a simple comment like: "This video helped me learn new words. Thank you, Bob!"

Mix It Up: Once you're comfortable, watch his "Q&A" or "Live Stream" videos. The English is a bit more spontaneous, which is great practice for moving from B1 to B2.

Be Patient: Don't worry if you don't understand 100%. Understanding 60-80% is great at B1. Your goal is to catch the main idea and some details.

Final Encouragement:
You have chosen a wonderful resource. Bob the Canadian is like a patient friend showing you the English-speaking world. If you follow this active listening plan, your listening comprehension and vocabulary will improve very quickly.

Good luck, and most importantly, have fun
